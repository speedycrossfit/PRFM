Who it's for

More than endurance.
Conditioning with purpose.

PRFM Capacity is built for people who want to improve how much work they can perform, how efficiently they move and how well they recover between efforts.

Drawing on traditional endurance training principles, the program combines structured training zones 1-5 with functional movements and targeted conditioning to develop both aerobic and anaerobic fitness.

From sustained aerobic efforts to high-intensity intervals and movement efficiency work, every session has a purpose. Build the ability to maintain output, recover between efforts and perform across a broad range of time and movement demands.

It's not just about working harder. It's about increasing your capacity to perform.

What you'll build

  1. 01

    Build your aerobic & anaerobic engine.

    Develop the ability to sustain longer efforts and produce repeatable high-intensity output through structured conditioning.

  2. 02

    Recover faster. Perform again.

    Improve your ability to recover between efforts, maintain performance across repeated intervals and tolerate greater volumes of training.

  3. 03

    Move better. Work more efficiently.

    Develop movement quality, technique and repetition speed so you can maintain output with less wasted energy, even as fatigue builds.

  4. 04

    Expand your work capacity.

    Build the ability to perform across different durations, intensities and movement types, whether you're training for HYROX, functional fitness competitions or personal performance goals.
