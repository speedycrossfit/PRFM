Who it's for

Built for strength.
Designed to perform.

PRFM Strong is built for people who want more from their training. We combine proven strength and bodybuilding principles with functional movements to build muscle, develop real-world strength and improve physical performance.

It's about building a body that doesn't just look strong, but performs that way too.

No random workouts. No unnecessary complexity. Just purposeful training designed to keep you moving forward.

A look inside your training.

Every session has a purpose. PRFM Strong combines full-body training with an emphasis on key movement patterns, building strength and muscle across the week.

Here's a snapshot of three sessions from the program. The full experience includes exercise demonstrations, scaling options and detailed training guidance.

Session 01

Full body / Lower body bias

  • Movement Prep / Warm Up
  • Back Squat
  • Shoulder Press
  • DB Lunges
  • DB RDL
  • Weighted Sit Up
  • Goblet Squat
  • Strict Pull Up

Session 02

Full body / Upper body bias

  • Movement Prep / Warm Up
  • Close Bench Press
  • DB Incline Press
  • Chin Up
  • Hip Thrust
  • Lat Pull Down
  • Dips

Session 03

Full body

  • Movement Prep / Warm Up
  • Deadlift
  • Leg Press
  • Bentover Row
  • Push Up
  • Hanging Leg Raises
  • DB Front Squat
  • Strict Pull Up
