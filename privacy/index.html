Information we collect and why

When you join our waitlist, we collect your first name and email to send the requested guide and PRFM launch updates. When you enquire or apply for coaching through Typeform, we receive the information you provide so we can respond and assess your application. Please avoid sending sensitive health information in general enquiries.

Providing information is voluntary, but we may be unable to respond or process a signup or application without the required details. Our website providers may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect the website.

Services we use

We use Vercel for hosting, Formspree for waitlist submissions, Typeform for coaching applications and Google Fonts for website fonts. These providers may process information outside New Zealand. We use appropriate safeguards where required for overseas processing. Their privacy policies describe their own practices:

  • Vercel
  • Formspree
  • Typeform
  • Google

Optional Meta advertising tracking

Only if you enable the advertising checkbox at the bottom of a page, we use Meta Pixel to measure page visits and confirmed waitlist signups, understand advertising performance and help show relevant PRFM advertising on Facebook and Instagram.

Meta may receive page URLs, event information, IP addresses, browser details and cookie identifiers, and associate this activity with a Meta account. We do not send names, email addresses, application answers, health details or muscle-building scorecard results as Pixel event data. Automatic event configuration is disabled in our implementation.

The checkbox is optional and off by default. Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. You can withdraw permission anytime by unchecking it; this stops further Pixel events and removes accessible PRFM-domain Meta cookies. Withdrawal does not undo information already sent to Meta. Browser privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track also disable our tracking. Refusing tracking does not affect forms or website access.

Read Meta's Privacy Policy and cookie information for details and additional controls.

Keeping and protecting information

We use reasonable safeguards and limit access to Ben Thompson and service providers who need the information for these purposes. We keep waitlist information while needed for the requested guide and launch updates, and enquiry or application information while needed to respond, assess an application or support a customer relationship.

We review records at least annually and securely delete or de-identify information no longer needed for a lawful purpose. Legal obligations may require certain records to be kept longer. Limited records may be retained to honour requests not to receive further updates.

Your choices and rights

Email hello@prfmfitness.com to stop waitlist updates, request access to or correction of your information, ask about deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also contact the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner. Other rights may apply depending on where you live.
